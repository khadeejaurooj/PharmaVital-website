Farah Khan

Research Scientist

Project: Development of a Personalized Immunotherapy for Cancer

Project Goal: Develop a new approach to cancer treatment using immunotherapy, where a patient's own immune system is harnessed to fight their specific tumor.

Phase 1 — Biomarker Discovery and Patient Selection: Identifying unique genetic markers on a patient's tumor cells and developing a diagnostic test to select suitable patients.
Phase 2 — Engineering Immune Cells and Pre-clinical Testing: Designing methods to extract and engineer a patient's T-cells to recognize and attack tumor markers, followed by pre-clinical safety and efficacy studies.
Phase 3 — Clinical Trials and Treatment Optimization: Designing protocols to test the personalized immunotherapy in patients and refining the process based on their responses.

Dr. Khan leverages her expertise in immunology, cell biology, and gene editing techniques, designing experiments, analyzing patient data, and ensuring the project adheres to ethical and regulatory guidelines.
