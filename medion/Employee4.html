Sarah Gill

Quality Control Analyst

Project: Implementing a New Dissolution Testing Method for a Sustained-Release Painkiller Tablet

Project Goal: Validate and implement a new dissolution testing method for a sustained-release painkiller tablet to ensure consistent drug release over time.

Phase 1 — Method Development and Validation: Reviewing current dissolution testing methods, developing a new method with analytical chemists, and validating its accuracy, precision, and robustness.
Phase 2 — Implementation and Ongoing Monitoring: Creating standard operating procedures, training QC personnel, integrating the method into routine batch testing, and flagging any batches outside established parameters.

Ms. Gill draws on her expertise in pharmaceutical analysis, quality control, and regulatory requirements to research, validate, document, and monitor the new method.
