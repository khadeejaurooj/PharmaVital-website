About Us

Our History

PharmaVital began in 1918, not in a gleaming laboratory, but in a converted farmhouse kitchen. Dr. Alia Sehgal, a brilliant young chemist, was driven by a personal tragedy: the loss of her brother to the Spanish Flu pandemic. Fueled by grief and a thirst for knowledge, she dedicated herself to researching infectious diseases.

Her early breakthrough came in the form of a refined quinine extract, significantly improving the effectiveness of the existing malaria treatment. This success allowed Dr. Sehgal to establish PharmaVital, named after the mysterious life-giving substance once believed to fill the cosmos.

However, PharmaVital hasn't been without its challenges. A Thalidomide-like incident in the 1970s led to stricter drug testing regulations and a period of self-reflection for the company. They emerged with a renewed commitment to safety and transparency. Today, PharmaVital is a global leader in the pharmaceutical industry, with research spanning oncology, neurology, infectious diseases, and rare genetic conditions, working collaboratively with universities and research institutions worldwide.
